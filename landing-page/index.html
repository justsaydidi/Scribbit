The Problem

The blank page is a liar.

You want to write more. You know it helps you think. You open a document, stare at it, then close it and check something else instead. This isn't a discipline problem. It's a design problem.

Every writing app is built for people who already write. Scribbit is built for the moment before that — when you're blank, distracted, and one friction point away from giving up.

How It Works

Three steps. Then just write.

01
Pick your session
Choose a prompt based on your interests, respond to something you've read, or just write freely. The app decides nothing for you — but it always gives you a starting point.
02
Write for 30 minutes
Full screen. No spell-check. No toolbars. No notifications. Just your words and a quiet timer counting down. The page doesn't judge. It just receives.
03
Get honest feedback
When you're done, ask the AI to review your writing. It tells you what's working, what to develop, and asks one question to push your thinking further. It never touches your spelling.
Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

AI Writing Prompts
Personalised to your interests. Never the same prompt twice. Always a starting point, never a constraint.
30-Minute Focus Timer
Long enough to write something real. Short enough to feel possible. The timer starts when you're ready.
Session Warm-Up
Two minutes of free writing to clear your head before the main session. Blank page paralysis doesn't stand a chance.
Writing Pattern Analysis
After several sessions, see the patterns across all your writing. What you do well. What you repeat. What to break.
Complete Privacy
Everything lives on your computer. Your writing is never sent to a server, never stored externally. Total ownership.
Choose Your AI
Bring your own key. Works with Gemini, Claude, OpenAI, or Mistral. Swap anytime in settings.
